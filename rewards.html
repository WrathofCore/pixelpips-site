REWARDS

Your antenna decides how well you hold the signal. STATIC barely holds. LOCKED never drops it. Pips that are switched on share their channel's stock by weight, not by headcount.

THE MATH

WEIGHTED SHARE

your share = your activated tier weight / total activated weight on your floor reward = accumulator growth × your weight - already claimed amount

A LOCKED pip holds its channel five times as well as a STATIC pip, so it earns five times the share. Hold a whole QUOTRON in the same wallet and your Pip's weight is boosted 1.25×.

DEAD AIR

PENDING POT

A channel with nothing switched on does not stop broadcasting. Incoming stock parks in that channel's pending pot, and the first pip to power on there claims the backlog.

TRANSFER RULE

REWARDS DO NOT FOLLOW THE NFT

What a pip has picked up is settled to its holder before it changes hands. You buy the receiver, not the seller's unclaimed stock, and nobody can front-run a distribution by buying the pip a block early.
