WHAT A PIP IS

RECEIVERA PIP IS A RECEIVERSmall, pixel, one antenna on its head. The antenna pulls in one channel and never changes.
CHANNELTEN CHANNELSOne per floor, NVDA to MSTR. Whatever channel a pip is tuned to, that is the stock it pays out.
SIGNALFOUR TIERS OF SIGNALHow well the antenna holds the channel. STATIC barely holds it. LOCKED never drops it. The tip light tells you which.
